Research Question & Thematic Fit:
Contemporary artificial intelligence architectures increasingly rely on massive, static training corpora and autoregressive sequence prediction over symbolic tokens. However, biological developmental cognition reveals that intelligent agents acquire physical intuition through active, grounded sensorimotor manipulation, reafference cancellation, and hypothesis-directed play. 

In this work, we investigate the fundamental cognitive question: Can an unprivileged embodied agent autonomously acquire, maintain, and adaptively update predictive structure about an interactive dynamical environment while actively discriminating competing physical hypotheses—without relying on Large Language Models or privileged simulator state?

Because Cognitive Systems Research explicitly emphasizes cognitive architectures, developmental systems, computational cognitive modeling, and sensorimotor learning, we believe the unified embodied architecture will be of strong interest to the journal's readership.

Key Empirical Contributions:
1. Online Structure Acquisition & Baseline Verification: An unprivileged predictive forward model operating via recursive parameter estimation and motor reafference cancellation, achieving an 8.8x forecasting MSE reduction over persistence across 7 baseline benchmarks (Cohen's d = 11.45).
2. Cross-World Adaptation: Audited evaluation across N = 50 deterministic seeds demonstrating robust cross-world transfer and rapid adaptation under sudden latent parameter shifts (A -> B -> C, T_epsilon = 16.32 +/- 0.62 steps).
3. Continual Multi-Schema Retention: A discrete schema memory store maintaining previously acquired forward models across alternating domains, achieving an empirical retention index of R = 0.9901 +/- 0.0141 (50/50 pass rate), effectively preventing domain interference compared to monolithic parameter overwriting (R = -72.97).
4. Active Epistemic Action Selection: Active inference driving targeted exploratory actions that maximize hypothesis divergence, accelerating structural hypothesis discrimination from a median of 17.0 steps (random babbling, 22.0% right-censoring) to 7.0 steps (active, 0% censoring) under a fair decoupled-RNG Kaplan-Meier survival analysis protocol (log-rank chi^2 = 74.98, p < 10^-17).
5. Sensitivity & Architectural Ablations: Systematic ablations characterizing the functional roles of directional EMA filtering, discrete schema retention, and epistemic action selection in preventing specific operational failure modes.
